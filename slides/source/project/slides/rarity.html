Now every portfolio from the decision slide becomes a quantum instance, 22 to 26 qubits each. For a 10 percent estimate, amplitude estimation needs 4 times fewer queries than Monte Carlo for the riskiest portfolio and 48 times fewer for the safest, growing like one over root p, exactly as theory says. The safer the grid, the bigger the win. To be fair we also compared against importance sampling, a classical trick that oversamples failures, tuned with knowledge of the answer. At 10 percent it is roughly a tie; because the quantum error falls like one over N, quantum is ahead for every portfolio once you ask for 5 percent or better. This is query complexity, not wall-clock time.
