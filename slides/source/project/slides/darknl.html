Sources: NL Hydro briefing to the PUB (Jan 2014); NL Hydro notices and Winter Readiness Report (2026); NL / ECCC facility GHG data (593-655 kt CO2e, 2022-2024)

Three facts frame the problem. DarkNL in 2014: about 190,000 customers lost power in a cold snap. Ten months ago, in January 2026, frazil ice shut the island’s biggest plant, Bay d’Espoir, for the first time since 1967, and Hydro asked everyone to prepare for rotating outages. The plant that keeps the lights on in those moments is Holyrood, which burns heavy oil and emits around 600,000 tonnes of CO2 a year. It is supposed to retire around 2030.
