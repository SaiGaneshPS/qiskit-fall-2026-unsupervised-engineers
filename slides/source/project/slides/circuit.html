The quantum component. We adapted IBM’s quantum credit-risk algorithm: plants play the loans that can default, and the weather is the hidden factor that makes failures happen together. Five qubits hold the AI’s net-load distribution in their amplitudes. One qubit per big plant holds its outage; the frazil shutdown is a rotation controlled by how cold the hour is, which is how correlated failures enter. A quantum weighted adder and comparator compute, for every combination at once, whether supply falls short, and flip a flag. Amplitude estimation then reads the flag’s probability with error falling like one over N instead of one over root N. The whole state preparation is 24 qubits.
