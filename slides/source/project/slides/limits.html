What the evidence supports, and what it does not

Supported (in our model)

  • AI beats the utility baseline on an unseen winter
  • Engine reproduces NL Hydro’s LOLH within ~10%
  • Baseboard-first heat pumps halve blackout risk
  • No-new-diesel retirement works at the link’s measured reliability
  • Circuits exact; 4x to 48x fewer queries than Monte Carlo
  • 3-qubit version beats Monte Carlo under Heron noise

Limits and next steps

  • Screening model: no transmission, hydro energy or imports
  • Four winters of demand; 2023-24 peaks disagree with Hydro
  • Simulators only so far; next: the IBM hardware run
  • Advantage is in queries, not seconds; deep circuits need fault tolerance
  • Importance sampling ties at 10%; next: combine it with QAE
  • Cold-snap parameters illustrative; next: hourly steps
To be clear about what we showed. Within our model, the AI, the validation, the heat-pump finding and the retirement finding are supported, and the quantum results are exact on simulators with a query advantage that grows as the grid gets safer. Limits: it is a screening model without transmission or hydro limits; we have four winters of demand data, and the 2023-24 peaks in the archive disagree with Hydro’s statements; everything quantum is simulated; the advantage is counted in queries, and deep circuits need fault-tolerant machines; tuned importance sampling ties us at 10 percent. Next steps are on the right.
