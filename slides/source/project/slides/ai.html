Data: NL Hydro island demand via Statistics Canada HFED (Sep 2022 to Nov 2026, 373k readings); ERA5 weather via Open-Meteo

The AI layer is gradient boosting on weather from three sites plus calendar features, trained on real NL Hydro demand. On the 2025-26 winter, which it never saw, it beats the linear degree-hour model utilities use. Trees cannot extrapolate, and the 1990 cold wave was 6 degrees colder than anything in our data, so we added a physics tail: below the coldest training temperature, demand keeps rising at the slope learned from the coldest hours. Trained only on mild hours, it halves the error on cold ones. The honest negative: the flash freeze of 4 February 2023 sits about 320 megawatts above every model. So the reliability engine does not trust point forecasts; it uses the model’s real out-of-sample error distribution.
