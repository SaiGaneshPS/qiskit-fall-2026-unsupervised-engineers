Lights on, oil off

Can St. John’s keep the heat on through the next DarkNL without burning oil?

AI-learned island demand + quantum amplitude estimation of winter blackout risk

[Team name] · [Team members] · Memorial University

Open with the cold. Everyone in this room remembers a winter night when the power went out or the radio said to turn down the heat. Our project asks a simple question with a hard answer: if we shut down the oil-fired plant at Holyrood and move homes to heat pumps, will the lights stay on in the next cold snap? We answer it with an AI trained on real grid data, a reliability engine checked against NL Hydro’s own numbers, and a quantum circuit that estimates blackout risk.
