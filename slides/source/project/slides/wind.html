St. John’s coldest hours are windy

60%

Wind turbines at the Fermeuse site would run at 60% of capacity in the 0.1% highest-demand hours, against 40% on average.

The wind that chills St. John’s could help heat it. Only 5% of the top 200 demand hours are calm.

ERA5 100 m wind, generic 3 MW turbine, cut-out below -20 C; icing not modelled, so an upper bound.

A finding we did not expect. In many places the coldest nights are calm, which is bad news for wind power. In St. John’s, the AI replay says the opposite: the highest-demand hours come with strong wind, so a wind farm at Fermeuse would produce more, not less, when we need it most. Caveat: we used reanalysis wind and a generic turbine without icing, so treat this as an upper bound.
