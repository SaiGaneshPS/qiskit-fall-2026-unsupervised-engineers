Baselines at every step: linear degree-hour model (AI), exact convolution and Monte Carlo (risk), tuned importance sampling, and NL Hydro’s own published results.

All quantum results are from simulators (Qiskit Aer, IBM Heron noise model) unless marked “hardware”.

The pipeline has five steps. Real weather since 1980 and four years of real island demand go into an AI model. It replays 46 winters on today’s grid, including its own forecast errors and the wind output of the same weather. A classical reliability engine turns that into blackout risk, and we check it against NL Hydro. The quantum part prepares every combination of demand and plant outage in one state, flags the shortfalls with a quantum adder, and amplitude estimation reads the probability. Every step has a baseline.
